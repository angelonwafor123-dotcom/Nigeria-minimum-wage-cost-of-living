# Nigeria-minimum-wage-cost-of-living
# Statistical Analysis: Effect of Minimum Wage on Cost of Living in Nigeria (2009–2023)

##  Executive Summary
This project investigates the empirical relationship between Nigeria's national minimum wage policy and the rising cost of living (proxied by the Consumer Price Index - CPI) over a 15-year period (2009–2023). Conducted as part of undergraduate research for a **B.Sc. in Statistics at Nnamdi Azikiwe University (UNIZIK)**, this study evaluates whether wage increments genuinely improve worker welfare or are neutralized by inflationary pressures (wage-price spiral theory).

---

## Key Research Metrics & Findings
* **Study Period:** 2009 – 2023 (Annual Time Series Data)
* **Variables Analyzed:** 
  * Independent Variable ($X$): National Minimum Wage ($\₦$)
  * Dependent Variable ($Y$): Consumer Price Index (CPI / Cost of Living Proxy)
* **Correlation Coefficient ($r$):** **0.762** ($p = 0.001$), indicating a strong positive relationship between minimum wage adjustments and CPI increases.
* **Coefficient of Determination ($R^2$):** **0.581**, meaning **58.1%** of the variation in cost of living during the period is explained by minimum wage adjustments, with the remaining 42.9% driven by external economic factors (inflation, exchange rate fluctuations, fuel subsidy removal).
* **Regression Model:** $\hat{Y} = -61.43 + 0.016X$ ($F = 18.025, p = 0.001$).

---

##  Statistical Methods & Tools Applied
* **Descriptive Statistics:** Evaluated central tendency, dispersion, and historical trends showing stagnant wage intervals versus steady, compounding CPI growth.
* **Inferential Statistics:**
  * **Pearson Correlation Analysis:** Tested the strength and direction of linear association between wages and price indexes.
  * **Simple Linear Regression Analysis:** Estimated parameters using the Least Squares approach, testing hypotheses via $t$-statistics and ANOVA ($F$-test).
* **Residual Diagnostics:**
  * **Normality Checks:** Evaluated standardized residuals via histograms and Normal P–P plots.
  * **Homoscedasticity:** Verified constant error variance through residual scatterplots.
* **Software:** SPSS & Microsoft Excel for data collation, modeling, and graphical diagnostics.
